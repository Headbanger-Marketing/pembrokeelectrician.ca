A useful assessment has a stated purpose and boundary

A condition concern, renovation decision or insurer request can each call for a different assessment. Agree which equipment and areas will be reviewed, whether testing is included and what written result will be provided. Concealed wiring and inaccessible equipment may limit conclusions. A general visit should not be represented as complete certification of everything in the property.

Prepare the scope for your Pembroke property

Collect receipts, ESA paperwork, inspection reports and seller disclosures that mention past work, then sketch which rooms and outbuildings they relate to. Photograph visible equipment, including any detached garage or workshop, from the outside.

Ask which items the visit will cover, whether any testing is included and how work without records will be described in the report. Ask that past work not be called approved or corrected unless that was truly established.

What should the assessment establish?

Going ahead with any further step the contractor proposes, and when, is for the homeowner to decide, and the report gives that step a short section of its own. The report also lists what was reviewed and what was out of reach. What was observed, what was tested and what was only seen from outside stay in three separate groups. Where a Pembroke property includes a detached garage or other outbuilding, its feeder and equipment are marked in or out of scope. No visit can turn old work into documented work, and the report's wording should not imply that it has.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

How far can one visit vouch for wiring someone else installed?

Only as far as the contractor can see and test, and the report should name where that limit falls. Wiring inside walls and equipment that cannot be reached leave some questions open.

Does a review of undocumented work have to be reported to ESA?

Whether anything must be notified depends on the work found and is for the contractor to explain. Ask what would trigger an ESA notification and who would file it before agreeing to a scope.

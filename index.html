Before work begins

Find out what was done before you build on top of it.

A property with a detached garage or workshop may have a feeder out to that building that a previous owner added, with no drawing, no paperwork and no label showing what it serves. Any home can hold earlier work like that.

Before anyone relies on that work or builds around it, gather whatever exists: old invoices, ESA paperwork, inspection reports and photographs of visible equipment. Then ask a Licensed Electrical Contractor what can be confirmed and what cannot.

What if I have no records of earlier electrical work?

Missing records are common and do not stop an enquiry. Describe what you can see and what you were told, and ask the contractor what an assessment can confirm and what would stay unverified.

Where can I look for ESA paperwork on earlier work?

Any ESA paperwork may be with the seller, the previous owner or the original contractor, so request it from them and keep what you find. A contractor can explain which notification or inspection records are worth looking for.

Does a garage feeder need a second look before I add a heater?

Where a garage or workshop is fed from the house, nobody may know what the feeder was built to carry. Ask the contractor to assess it before any new equipment is planned for that building.

What happens if the assessment finds work nobody can explain?

The contractor should describe what was found in writing, separate what can be verified from what cannot, and propose options. Whether anything needs correcting, and how, is decided in that written scope, not assumed.
